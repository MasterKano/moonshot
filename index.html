How to read the timeline

Pilot events, screen events, and the Norwegian shelf, newest first. The page opens on the last 12 months, with material events and setbacks. Background shelf items stay behind Show background. Upcoming switches to the coming up list, soonest first. The chart above the list uses the same filters. It shows up to 25 companies, the ones with the most events in the view. Watchlist shows every pinned name. A Today line marks the current day.

Material
Large enough to matter for the company.
Setback
A dry hole, a delay, or another step backward.
Background
A shelf change that is neither material nor a setback.
Also affects
The same event touches other companies in the screen. Context only.
Past guided date
On Upcoming, a guided date with no announcement. The original guidance is in the tooltip.
Pilot detail
Open a pilot row for the risk removed, the stage reached, and the other register labels.
Chart
One lane per company. A filled marker has happened. An open marker is still ahead. A dashed outline means the date is a month or a wider window, not a single day. A crowded week shows up to three markers, then a count for the rest. Months sit on the top axis. The chart scrolls sideways inside its own box.
Historical context
r5, r20, and r60 are the share-price change after the event. They are context, not a signal.
Source
A link icon opens the filing or release. The cell stays empty when none is on file.
